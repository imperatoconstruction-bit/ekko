New book by Brandon Imperato

The Church Isn't For Me.

Rediscovering what the church was meant to be.

Join Book UpdatesView Resources

Coming Summer 2026

It's not about what I get. It's about who I become and who I'm sent to.

The church was never meant to be about us — it was meant to be about Him and His mission.

The big idea

Set apart. Sent on purpose. Called out.

The Church Isn't For Me is a call to rediscover the biblical purpose of the church — not as a consumer experience, but as a people formed by Jesus and sent into His mission.

It challenges the idea that church exists primarily to meet our preferences and invites readers back into community, accountability, mission, and spiritual formation.

Inside the book

What readers will discover.
