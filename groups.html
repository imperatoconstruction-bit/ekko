How groups work

From connection to commitment.

Groups help people move from watching from a distance to actually being known. Some groups may meet locally, some digitally, and some around specific rhythms like prayer, Scripture, marriage, parenting, young adults, or leadership.

The goal is simple: help people walk with Jesus and with each other.

Group experience

Simple rhythms that form people.

01

Gather

Meet weekly, biweekly, or monthly around a shared spiritual rhythm.

02

Share

Have honest conversations about Scripture, prayer, life, and obedience.

03

Pray

Carry each other’s burdens and celebrate answered prayers together.

04

Go

Live sent into homes, churches, workplaces, and communities.
