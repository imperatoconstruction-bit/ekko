Who EKKO is for

The ones longing for more than surface-level faith.

EKKO is for people who love Jesus, or are learning how to, and know they were not designed to walk alone.

It is for the believer who wants real community, the new believer looking for guidance, the parent trying to lead their home, the young adult seeking purpose, and the one who feels surrounded by people but still spiritually alone.
