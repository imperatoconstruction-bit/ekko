How it works

Simple. Focused. Spiritually meaningful.

Share

Post a request honestly, publicly or anonymously, and invite the community to pray with you.

Pray

Let someone know they are not alone by praying, responding, and standing with them.

Testify

Mark prayers as answered and let someone else borrow faith from what God has done.
